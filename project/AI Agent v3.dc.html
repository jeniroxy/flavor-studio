What it does

Real work, not chit-chat.

Ingredient sourcing
"Find a non-GMO starch with no soy under $2.10/kg." It searches your library and supplier feeds and ranks the matches.
Claim & label checks
Validates nutrient content claims against 21 CFR 101 and flags what you qualify for before you print the label.
What-if costing
Model ingredient swaps, supplier changes and batch scaling — see the cost and margin impact before touching the formula.
Nutrition compare
Side-by-side nutrient panels across versions — see exactly what a reformulation changes, per serving and per 100 g.
Allergen watch
Ask what the big-9 exposure is on any formula and get the mandatory "Contains" statement, cross-contact risks included.
Reformulation ideas
Propose swaps to hit a sugar, sodium or cost target while holding the sensory score — as reviewable draft versions.
